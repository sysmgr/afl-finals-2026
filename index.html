This page checks for new numbers every minute — there is nothing to refresh. The lines are the bookmaker’s, captured every three hours.

During a final the win chance is recalculated at each break from the margin, the time left and the model’s pre-game margin, and every club’s premiership chance is re-weighted by it; at full time the result is fixed and the bracket rebuilt. Ratings don’t learn from the score itself — a side ten goals up isn’t rated any better for its next final.

# Swans investigation

Summary checked 2026-09-26 08:45 AEST. Typed from named reports; not an input to the forecast.

## Where it stands

Victoria Police's investigation into an alleged sexual assault at the team hotel on 17 August is ongoing. No one has been arrested or charged, and reports have not suggested any player is accused of the assault. Detectives interviewed four of the five stood-down players on 2 September; Riley Bice is not under investigation, and police said then that they did not expect to give a further update for about eight weeks, so roughly late October. The club bans ran to the end of Sydney's season, which finished with the preliminary final on 18 September, and the club says the five players' futures will be worked through now.
